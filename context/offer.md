# Lancara Offer

Lancara is an opportunity-intelligence company for B2B service businesses.

It helps sales teams identify companies that may need their services, understand why the timing may be relevant, prioritize the strongest accounts, and prepare the next commercial action.

Lancara is designed to combine company data, public signals, customer-specific qualification, AI-assisted research, workflow automation, and human judgment into one operating system for account discovery and sales preparation.

## Core promise

Lancara helps customers answer:

1. Which companies should we prioritize?
2. Why are they relevant to our business?
3. What evidence suggests they may need our services?
4. Why may the timing be important now?
5. Which stakeholders should we approach?
6. What should our sales team do next?

The objective is not to produce the largest possible lead list.

The objective is to consistently surface a smaller number of credible, explainable, and actionable commercial opportunities.

## Who Lancara serves

Lancara is designed for B2B service businesses that depend on winning new company clients.

Potential customer segments include:

- Specialist recruitment agencies
- Software-development companies
- IT service providers
- Data and AI consultancies
- Cybersecurity firms
- Cloud and infrastructure providers
- Engineering and technical service firms
- Professional-services companies
- Other relationship-led B2B service businesses

Each customer is intended to use an industry-specific and service-specific opportunity-intelligence playbook.

Lancara's long-term platform is designed around six connected capabilities. The descriptions below define the intended company offer; they do not imply that every capability is operational today.

### 1. Customer and ICP intelligence

Lancara's customer-specific model is designed to capture how each customer wins business, including:

- Services offered
- Target industries
- Target company characteristics
- Geographic coverage
- Preferred deal size
- Commercial requirements
- Excluded accounts
- Buyer personas
- Relevant case studies
- Successful previous customers
- Competitive strengths
- Delivery capacity
- Existing customers and active prospects

Lancara does not rely only on broad filters such as industry, company size, or location.

Its qualification approach is designed around:

- Business fit
- Problem relevance
- Timing
- Commercial potential
- Customer capability
- Evidence quality
- Actionability

During the current phase, these inputs and rules are gathered and applied manually. A future platform may store and operationalize validated versions of them.

### 2. Total addressable market sourcing

The planned platform will help customers build and maintain a relevant account universe.

Future capabilities may include:

- Sourcing companies from approved public and connected data sources
- Importing customer-provided account lists
- Normalizing company names, domains, industries, and locations
- Detecting duplicate accounts
- Excluding existing customers and unsuitable companies
- Categorizing accounts using customer-specific definitions
- Identifying new companies entering the customer's target market
- Refreshing the qualified account universe on an agreed schedule

The intended result is not a static database. It is a customer-specific view of the market that can change as companies, evidence, and customer priorities change.

Recurring refresh and connected-source collection are planned capabilities, not current operational claims.

### 3. Signal detection and opportunity patterns

Lancara's research process is designed to evaluate evidence that may indicate a company is experiencing a relevant business need.

Possible signals include:

- Hiring activity
- Repeated or difficult-to-fill vacancies
- Leadership changes
- Funding events
- Geographic expansion
- New offices or facilities
- Product launches
- Technology initiatives
- Regulatory or compliance changes
- Mergers and acquisitions
- Rapid headcount growth
- New business units
- Procurement activity
- Public tenders
- Technology-stack changes
- Customer-specific operational events

Lancara does not treat one signal as confirmed buying intent.

It combines related evidence into opportunity patterns. Examples may include:

- Specialist hiring bottleneck
- Rapid team expansion
- New-market buildout
- Leadership-led transformation
- Technology-modernization initiative
- Regulatory-readiness requirement
- Outsourcing-capacity gap
- Post-funding operational expansion

Each pattern should be evaluated against the customer's services and commercial requirements. During validation, this interpretation remains manual and human-reviewed. The planned platform may later assist with recurring detection and draft classification.

### 4. Account research and qualification

During validation, human-reviewed research can transform fragmented company information into structured commercial intelligence.

For a relevant account, an opportunity brief may include:

- Company profile
- Business context
- Verified source evidence
- Signal history
- Opportunity pattern
- Customer-specific fit assessment
- Probable business problem
- Relevant customer service
- Timing and urgency
- Commercial opportunity assessment
- Evidence-confidence level
- Potential disqualifying factors
- Questions requiring further verification
- Recommended monitoring or action status

Possible recommendations include:

- Contact now
- Research further
- Monitor
- Postpone
- Exclude

Commercial opportunity potential must remain separate from evidence confidence. Any future scoring model must remain explainable.

Users should be able to see:

- Which dimensions influenced the result
- Which evidence supports the conclusion
- What remains uncertain
- Why the account was prioritized
- What could make the recommendation incorrect

Low-confidence evidence must not produce an unsupported contact-now recommendation.

### 5. Stakeholder and sales assistance

Lancara is designed to help sales teams prepare for relevant conversations.

Human-reviewed briefs may recommend:

- Likely decision-makers
- Potential champions
- Operational users
- Technical stakeholders
- Procurement roles
- Recommended contact order
- Relevant case studies
- Conversation objectives
- Discovery questions
- Outreach angles
- Email drafts
- LinkedIn-message drafts
- WhatsApp-message drafts
- Call openers
- Meeting-preparation briefs
- Follow-up drafts

Lancara assists the salesperson but does not replace human judgment.

The user remains responsible for reviewing, editing, approving, and sending commercial communication. Lancara does not currently perform automated outreach.

### 6. Workflow orchestration and activation

Workflow orchestration is a planned platform capability. It is intended to connect approved opportunity intelligence to a customer's existing sales workflow.

Future capabilities may include:

- Scheduled account monitoring
- Signal-change alerts
- Opportunity queues
- Human-review checkpoints
- Sales-representative assignment
- Weekly opportunity digests
- CRM enrichment
- CRM updates
- Spreadsheet import and export
- Webhooks
- Internal notifications
- Task creation
- Customer-specific approval workflows
- Delivery through dashboards, email, or connected systems

Lancara should complement the customer's CRM, not attempt to replace it.

The CRM can remain the system of record for:

- Contacts
- Communication history
- Deals
- Pipeline stages
- Proposals
- Tasks
- Revenue

The planned Lancara platform can become the intelligence and qualification layer that helps determine what should enter the sales workflow and why. CRM synchronization, dashboards, and connected activation are not currently operational.

## Lancara playbooks

Lancara is designed to support customers through ready-to-run playbooks rather than requiring them to build complex workflows from scratch.

During validation, a playbook is a customer-specific manual research and qualification specification. Future software may operationalize playbooks only after their rules have been tested with customers.

Each playbook defines:

- Target account universe
- Relevant services
- Signal sources
- Opportunity patterns
- Qualification rules
- Evidence requirements
- Exclusions
- Stakeholders
- Recommended actions
- Feedback rules
- Success metrics

Potential playbooks include:

### Recruitment Hiring Demand Playbook

Identifies companies showing credible specialist hiring demand and evaluates whether external recruitment support may be relevant.

### Software Project Opportunity Playbook

Identifies companies showing expansion, technology, operational, or leadership signals that may create demand for software-development services.

### Cybersecurity Readiness Playbook

Identifies companies facing regulatory, infrastructure, leadership, or incident-related conditions that may create demand for cybersecurity support.

### Cloud Modernization Playbook

Identifies organizations showing infrastructure growth, migration, hiring, or digital-transformation signals.

### Data and AI Transformation Playbook

Identifies companies investing in data teams, AI initiatives, analytics infrastructure, or related leadership.

### Account Expansion Playbook

Monitors existing customers and product users for cross-sell, upsell, geographic-expansion, or new-stakeholder opportunities.

### Champion and Leadership Tracking Playbook

Tracks important contacts and leadership changes across target and former customer accounts.

### Inbound Qualification Playbook

Enriches and evaluates inbound enquiries, routes them to the correct team, and prepares a relevant first response.

These examples describe possible playbooks. They are not all implemented or validated.

## Inbound intelligence

Inbound intelligence is a planned capability. It may help customers qualify incoming forms, referrals, event leads, and enquiries by:

- Identifying the company
- Enriching the account
- Understanding the request
- Matching the need to customer capabilities
- Estimating commercial attractiveness
- Detecting existing relationships
- Identifying relevant case studies
- Recommending the owner
- Preparing qualification questions
- Drafting the initial response
- Routing the opportunity into the existing sales workflow

The intended objective is to help the customer respond faster and with better context.

## Outbound intelligence

Lancara is designed to help customers identify and prepare high-quality outbound opportunities.

During validation, this work is manual and human-reviewed. Future capabilities may include:

- Building target-account lists
- Detecting relevant signals
- Qualifying accounts
- Prioritizing timing
- Mapping stakeholders
- Generating account briefs
- Recommending outreach channels
- Preparing personalized drafts
- Routing approved opportunities to sales teams or sequencing tools

Lancara should prioritize relevant and well-researched outreach over mass automated messaging.

Human approval should remain the default for high-value B2B sales. Automated outreach is outside the current scope.

## Customer feedback and learning

During calibration and pilot delivery, Lancara is designed to learn from customer decisions and sales outcomes.

Customers may provide feedback such as:

- Approve
- Edit
- Reject
- Postpone
- Already pursuing
- Existing customer
- Request deeper research

Feedback may include:

- Rejection reason
- Whether the account was contacted
- Whether a response was received
- Whether a meeting was created
- Whether an opportunity entered the pipeline
- Whether a proposal was sent
- Whether the opportunity was won or lost

This feedback can help Lancara learn:

- Which signals customers trust
- Which opportunity patterns are useful
- Which accounts convert
- Which situations should be excluded
- Which recommendations require more evidence
- Which customer strengths create the highest commercial fit

The objective is not to create an unexplained autonomous scoring system.

The objective is to improve customer-specific qualification while preserving transparency and human control.

Customers should not be required to become GTM engineers.

Depending on the validation stage and future service level, Lancara may provide:

- Discovery and workflow design
- ICP configuration
- Playbook setup
- Data-source configuration
- Account-list preparation
- Signal and scoring calibration
- CRM field mapping
- Workflow implementation
- Team onboarding
- Weekly calibration
- Quality assurance
- Ongoing optimization

The level of service may depend on the customer plan and operational complexity.

Lancara should prioritize fast time to value. Customers should receive useful opportunity intelligence before being asked to configure complex systems independently.

## Commercial model

### Current validation offers

#### Free Opportunity Calibration

A limited engagement used to configure the customer profile and demonstrate the quality of Lancara's opportunity intelligence.

The current design includes:

- One 30-minute configuration session
- Three researched, human-reviewed opportunity briefs
- One 20-minute review session
- Delivery within five business days after complete onboarding
- No payment

#### Four-Week Founding Pilot

A time-limited paid engagement combining customer-specific research, human-reviewed opportunities, workflow calibration, and performance measurement.

The current test is Rp4 million, requested upfront, for:

- One configured playbook
- 10–15 accounts researched weekly
- 5–8 complete human-reviewed briefs weekly
- Weekly calibration
- Up to two participating customer team members
- A maximum of two simultaneous paid customers

This offer is a commercial validation test, not proof of established demand.

### Planned future service levels

The following service levels are possible future offers. They are not currently operational.

#### Managed Playbook

An ongoing service in which Lancara operates and improves one or more validated opportunity-intelligence playbooks for the customer.

#### Platform Subscription

Planned software access for internal teams to monitor accounts, review opportunities, provide feedback, and activate approved recommendations.

#### Enterprise Implementation

A possible customized engagement involving multiple data sources, CRM integration, account models, team workflows, governance, and ongoing support.

Future pricing may depend on:

- Number of playbooks
- Number of monitored accounts
- Research and enrichment requirements
- Number of users
- Delivery frequency
- Integration complexity
- Human-review requirements
- Level of implementation and support

Lancara should price around customer value and operational scope rather than exposing customers to complicated data-credit calculations whenever possible.
