\#\# Lancara Offer

Lancara is an opportunity-intelligence platform for B2B service companies.

It helps sales teams identify companies that are likely to need their services, understand why the timing may be relevant, prioritize the strongest accounts, and prepare the next commercial action.

Lancara combines company data, public signals, customer-specific qualification, AI-assisted research, workflow automation, and human judgment into one operating system for account discovery and sales preparation.

\#\# Core promise

Lancara helps customers answer:

1\. Which companies should we prioritize?  
2\. Why are they relevant to our business?  
3\. What evidence suggests they may need our services?  
4\. Why may the timing be important now?  
5\. Which stakeholders should we approach?  
6\. What should our sales team do next?

The objective is not to produce the largest possible lead list.

The objective is to consistently surface a smaller number of credible, explainable, and actionable commercial opportunities.

\#\# Who Lancara serves

Lancara is designed for B2B service companies that depend on winning new business accounts.

Potential customer segments include:

\- Specialist recruitment agencies  
\- Software-development companies  
\- IT service providers  
\- Data and AI consultancies  
\- Cybersecurity firms  
\- Cloud and infrastructure providers  
\- Engineering and technical service firms  
\- Professional-services companies  
\- Other relationship-led B2B service businesses

Each customer is supported through an industry-specific and service-specific opportunity-intelligence playbook.

\#\# The Lancara platform

Lancara is built around six connected capabilities.

\#\#\# 1\. Customer and ICP intelligence

Lancara learns how each customer wins business.

The platform captures:

\- Services offered  
\- Target industries  
\- Target company characteristics  
\- Geographic coverage  
\- Preferred deal size  
\- Commercial requirements  
\- Excluded accounts  
\- Buyer personas  
\- Relevant case studies  
\- Successful previous customers  
\- Competitive strengths  
\- Delivery capacity  
\- Existing customers and active prospects

Lancara does not rely only on broad filters such as industry, company size, or location.

It builds a customer-specific qualification model based on:

\- Business fit  
\- Problem relevance  
\- Timing  
\- commercial potential  
\- Customer capability  
\- Evidence quality  
\- Actionability

\#\#\# 2\. Total addressable market sourcing

Lancara helps customers build and maintain a relevant account universe.

The platform can:

\- Source companies from approved public and connected data sources  
\- Import customer-provided account lists  
\- Normalize company names, domains, industries, and locations  
\- Detect duplicate accounts  
\- Exclude existing customers and unsuitable companies  
\- Categorize accounts using customer-specific definitions  
\- Identify new companies entering the customer’s target market  
\- Continuously refresh the qualified account universe

The result is not a static database.

It is a customer-specific view of the market that changes as companies, signals, and customer priorities change.

\#\#\# 3\. Signal detection and opportunity patterns

Lancara monitors evidence that may indicate a company is experiencing a relevant business need.

Possible signals include:

\- Hiring activity  
\- Repeated or difficult-to-fill vacancies  
\- Leadership changes  
\- Funding events  
\- Geographic expansion  
\- New offices or facilities  
\- Product launches  
\- Technology initiatives  
\- Regulatory or compliance changes  
\- Mergers and acquisitions  
\- Rapid headcount growth  
\- New business units  
\- Procurement activity  
\- Public tenders  
\- Technology-stack changes  
\- Customer-specific operational events

Lancara does not treat one signal as confirmed buying intent.

It combines several related signals into opportunity patterns.

Examples may include:

\- Specialist hiring bottleneck  
\- Rapid team expansion  
\- New-market buildout  
\- Leadership-led transformation  
\- Technology-modernization initiative  
\- Regulatory-readiness requirement  
\- Outsourcing-capacity gap  
\- Post-funding operational expansion

Each pattern is evaluated against the customer’s services and commercial requirements.

\#\#\# 4\. Account research and qualification

Lancara transforms fragmented company information into structured commercial intelligence.

For every relevant account, the platform can produce:

\- Company profile  
\- Business context  
\- Verified source evidence  
\- Signal history  
\- Opportunity pattern  
\- Customer-specific fit assessment  
\- Probable business problem  
\- Relevant customer service  
\- Timing and urgency  
\- Opportunity score  
\- Evidence-confidence level  
\- Potential disqualifying factors  
\- Questions requiring further verification  
\- Recommended monitoring or action status

Possible recommendations include:

\- Contact now  
\- Research further  
\- Monitor  
\- Postpone  
\- Exclude

The opportunity score must remain explainable.

Users should be able to see:

\- Which dimensions influenced the result  
\- Which evidence supports the conclusion  
\- What remains uncertain  
\- Why the account was prioritized  
\- What could make the recommendation incorrect

\#\#\# 5\. Stakeholder and sales assistance

Lancara helps sales teams prepare for a relevant conversation.

For qualified opportunities, the platform can recommend:

\- Likely decision-makers  
\- Potential champions  
\- Operational users  
\- Technical stakeholders  
\- Procurement roles  
\- Recommended contact order  
\- Relevant case studies  
\- Conversation objectives  
\- Discovery questions  
\- Outreach angles  
\- Email drafts  
\- LinkedIn-message drafts  
\- WhatsApp-message drafts  
\- Call openers  
\- Meeting-preparation briefs  
\- Follow-up drafts

Lancara assists the salesperson but does not replace human judgment.

The user remains responsible for reviewing, editing, approving, and sending commercial communication.

\#\#\# 6\. Workflow orchestration and activation

Lancara connects opportunity intelligence to the customer’s existing sales workflow.

The platform may support:

\- Scheduled account monitoring  
\- Signal-change alerts  
\- Opportunity queues  
\- Human-review checkpoints  
\- Sales-representative assignment  
\- Weekly opportunity digests  
\- CRM enrichment  
\- CRM updates  
\- Spreadsheet import and export  
\- Webhooks  
\- Internal notifications  
\- Task creation  
\- Customer-specific approval workflows  
\- Delivery through dashboards, email, or connected systems

Lancara should complement the customer’s CRM, not attempt to replace it.

The CRM can remain the system of record for:

\- Contacts  
\- Communication history  
\- Deals  
\- Pipeline stages  
\- Proposals  
\- Tasks  
\- Revenue

Lancara becomes the intelligence and qualification layer that helps determine what should enter the sales workflow and why.

\#\# Lancara playbooks

Customers use Lancara through ready-to-run playbooks rather than building complex workflows from scratch.

A playbook is a customer-specific research and qualification system designed around a commercial situation.

Each playbook defines:

\- Target account universe  
\- Relevant services  
\- Signal sources  
\- Opportunity patterns  
\- Qualification rules  
\- Evidence requirements  
\- Exclusions  
\- Stakeholders  
\- Recommended actions  
\- Feedback rules  
\- Success metrics

Example playbooks may include:

\#\#\# Recruitment Hiring Demand Playbook

Identifies companies showing credible specialist hiring demand and evaluates whether external recruitment support may be relevant.

\#\#\# Software Project Opportunity Playbook

Identifies companies showing expansion, technology, operational, or leadership signals that may create demand for software-development services.

\#\#\# Cybersecurity Readiness Playbook

Identifies companies facing regulatory, infrastructure, leadership, or incident-related conditions that may create demand for cybersecurity support.

\#\#\# Cloud Modernization Playbook

Identifies organizations showing infrastructure growth, migration, hiring, or digital-transformation signals.

\#\#\# Data and AI Transformation Playbook

Identifies companies investing in data teams, AI initiatives, analytics infrastructure, or related leadership.

\#\#\# Account Expansion Playbook

Monitors existing customers and product users for cross-sell, upsell, geographic-expansion, or new-stakeholder opportunities.

\#\#\# Champion and Leadership Tracking Playbook

Tracks important contacts and leadership changes across target and former customer accounts.

\#\#\# Inbound Qualification Playbook

Enriches and evaluates inbound enquiries, routes them to the correct team, and prepares a relevant first response.

\#\# Inbound intelligence

Lancara can help customers qualify incoming opportunities.

For inbound forms, referrals, event leads, and enquiries, the platform may:

\- Identify the company  
\- Enrich the account  
\- Understand the request  
\- Match the need to customer capabilities  
\- Estimate commercial attractiveness  
\- Detect existing relationships  
\- Identify relevant case studies  
\- Recommend the owner  
\- Prepare qualification questions  
\- Draft the initial response  
\- Route the opportunity into the existing sales workflow

The objective is to help the customer respond faster and with better context.

\#\# Outbound intelligence

Lancara can help customers identify and prepare high-quality outbound opportunities.

The platform may:

\- Build target-account lists  
\- Detect relevant signals  
\- Qualify accounts  
\- Prioritize timing  
\- Map stakeholders  
\- Generate account briefs  
\- Recommend outreach channels  
\- Prepare personalized drafts  
\- Route approved opportunities to sales teams or sequencing tools

Lancara should prioritize relevant and well-researched outreach over mass automated messaging.

Human approval should remain the default for high-value B2B sales.

\#\# Account-based marketing

For customers selling into a smaller number of high-value accounts, Lancara may support account-based workflows.

These may include:

\- Account tiering  
\- Buying-committee mapping  
\- Account-specific signal monitoring  
\- Multi-stakeholder research  
\- Marketing and sales coordination  
\- Campaign-trigger recommendations  
\- Territory planning  
\- Strategic-account alerts  
\- Engagement and opportunity reporting

\#\# CRM and data enrichment

Lancara can improve existing account and contact records.

The platform may:

\- Standardize company records  
\- Normalize industries and job titles  
\- Detect duplicates  
\- Refresh company information  
\- Identify stale records  
\- Add signal and research fields  
\- Track last-verification dates  
\- Add customer-specific qualification categories  
\- Recommend follow-up actions  
\- Sync validated information back to the CRM

Data provenance should remain visible.

Users should be able to understand where information came from and when it was last verified.

\#\# Customer feedback and learning

Lancara improves through customer decisions and sales outcomes.

Customers can provide feedback such as:

\- Approve  
\- Edit  
\- Reject  
\- Postpone  
\- Already pursuing  
\- Existing customer  
\- Request deeper research

Feedback may include:

\- Rejection reason  
\- Whether the account was contacted  
\- Whether a response was received  
\- Whether a meeting was created  
\- Whether an opportunity entered the pipeline  
\- Whether a proposal was sent  
\- Whether the opportunity was won or lost

This helps Lancara learn:

\- Which signals customers trust  
\- Which opportunity patterns are useful  
\- Which accounts convert  
\- Which situations should be excluded  
\- Which recommendations require more evidence  
\- Which customer strengths create the highest commercial fit

The objective is not to create an unexplained autonomous scoring system.

The objective is to improve customer-specific qualification while preserving transparency and human control.

\#\# Service and implementation model

Lancara combines software with customer-specific implementation.

Customers should not be required to become GTM engineers.

Lancara may provide:

\- Discovery and workflow design  
\- ICP configuration  
\- Playbook setup  
\- Data-source configuration  
\- Account-list preparation  
\- Signal and scoring calibration  
\- CRM field mapping  
\- Workflow implementation  
\- Team onboarding  
\- Weekly calibration  
\- Quality assurance  
\- Ongoing optimization

The level of service may depend on the customer plan and operational complexity.

Lancara should prioritize fast time to value.

Customers should receive useful opportunity intelligence before being asked to configure complex systems independently.

\#\# Product principles

\- Vertical-aware  
\- Customer-specific  
\- Evidence-first  
\- Explainable  
\- Human-controlled  
\- Workflow-compatible  
\- Outcome-oriented  
\- Modular

\- A bulk-email platform

\#\# Commercial model

Lancara may offer several service levels.

\#\#\# Opportunity Calibration

A limited engagement used to configure the customer profile and demonstrate the quality of Lancara’s opportunity intelligence.

\#\#\# Concierge Pilot

A time-limited implementation combining customer-specific research, human-reviewed opportunities, workflow calibration, and performance measurement.

\#\#\# Managed Playbook

An ongoing service in which Lancara operates and improves one or more opportunity-intelligence playbooks for the customer.

\#\#\# Platform Subscription

Software access for internal teams to monitor accounts, review opportunities, provide feedback, and activate approved recommendations.

\#\#\# Enterprise Implementation

A customized engagement involving multiple data sources, CRM integration, account models, team workflows, governance, and ongoing support.

Pricing may depend on:

\- Number of playbooks  
\- Number of monitored accounts  
\- Research and enrichment requirements  
\- Number of users  
\- Delivery frequency  
\- Integration complexity  
\- Human-review requirements  
\- Level of implementation and support

Lancara should price around customer value and operational scope rather than exposing customers to complicated data-credit calculations whenever possible.
