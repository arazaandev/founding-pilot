## **Lancara Values**

### **1\. Evidence before claims**

Recommendations must be supported by identifiable evidence.

Lancara should show:

* What was observed  
* Where it came from  
* When it was observed  
* What Lancara inferred from it  
* What remains unknown

### **2\. Human judgment remains essential**

Automation can collect, organize, and summarize information.

Humans should remain responsible for:

* Final qualification  
* Commercial interpretation  
* Evidence verification  
* Stakeholder prioritization  
* Outreach decisions  
* Customer relationships

### **3\. Relevance over volume**

A small number of credible opportunities is more valuable than a large list of weak leads.

Lancara should optimize for:

* Customer relevance  
* Actionability  
* Evidence quality  
* Commercial fit  
* Timing

Not raw lead volume.

### **4\. Honest uncertainty**

Lancara must not pretend that a signal proves buying intent.

Every opportunity should make room for:

* Alternative explanations  
* Missing information  
* Weak evidence  
* Disqualifying factors  
* Questions that still require confirmation

### **5\. Customer-specific intelligence**

The same account may be valuable to one customer and irrelevant to another.

Qualification should consider:

* Customer specialization  
* Service capability  
* Geographic coverage  
* Commercial requirements  
* Case studies  
* Delivery capacity  
* Existing relationships

### **6\. Build manually before automating**

Lancara should first understand the workflow through real delivery.

Automation should only be introduced after:

* The process is repeatable  
* The output is useful  
* Customers act on it  
* Customers are willing to pay  
* The economics are understood

### **7\. Protect customer trust**

Lancara should avoid:

* Spam  
* Fabricated personalization  
* Unverified claims  
* Misleading scores  
* Unnecessary personal-data collection  
* Automated actions that may damage customer reputation

### **8\. Learn from behaviour**

Compliments and hypothetical interest are not enough.

Strong validation comes from customers who:

* Review opportunities  
* Reject or approve them honestly  
* Contact selected accounts  
* Share outcomes  
* Invest time  
* Pay for continued delivery

### **9\. Remain vertical-flexible**

Lancara is not permanently defined by one industry.

The core system should remain reusable:

> Customer ICP → Evidence → Signals → Opportunity hypothesis → Qualification → Recommended action → Feedback

Industry-specific logic should be implemented through separate playbooks
