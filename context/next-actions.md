# Lancara Next Actions

Last updated: 5 August 2026

## Immediate objective

Determine whether specialist recruitment agencies or software and IT service firms provide the stronger initial market for Lancara.

Do not begin another broad product-development phase before obtaining behavioural evidence.

## Priority 1 — Complete Founder Discovery Operations

Create and use:

- Discovery-call guide
- Agency or company qualification checklist
- Calibration onboarding template
- Objection and verbatim-language log
- Opportunity-feedback record
- Three-call synthesis framework
- Validation gates and failure criteria
- Manual delivery-economics tracker
- Outreach tracker

Keep these as short operational documents or spreadsheets.

## Priority 2 — Apply public consistency fixes

Correct the current sample and website where necessary:

- Keep evidence confidence separate from the commercial score
- Replace language that implies fully continuous automated monitoring when the current process is recurring and human-reviewed

Avoid further broad redesign.

## Priority 3 — Build the comparison target lists

Create two category-verified lists.

### Specialist recruitment

Target ten employer-facing specialist agencies across technology and digital, engineering and manufacturing, financial services, and healthcare and life sciences.

### Software and IT services

Target ten firms across custom software, data and AI, cloud, cybersecurity, systems integration, and managed IT services.

Do not score invalid-category companies.

## Priority 4 — Conduct comparable discovery interviews

Target four specialist recruitment conversations and four software or IT service conversations.

Use the same core questions so results can be compared.

Record:

- Exact customer language
- Current acquisition process
- Research time
- Account-selection criteria
- Credible timing signals
- Rejection reasons
- Existing tools and workarounds
- Decision authority
- Willingness to review briefs
- Reaction to Rp4 million

## Priority 5 — Complete Gate A synthesis

After at least three qualified calls in a vertical, document repeated pain, contradicted assumptions, customer terminology, strongest current workaround, evidence customers trust, information needed before outreach, offer objections, changes required before calibration, and elements that should remain unchanged.

Three calls are a messaging gate, not demand validation.

## Priority 6 — Build Research Delivery Operations

After early interviews, create:

- Opportunity-research SOP
- Evidence standard
- Human-review checklist
- Opportunity-brief template
- Source and freshness rules
- Fact-versus-inference rules
- Duplicate and exclusion rules
- Production-time tracking

Complete this before producing the first real calibration briefs.

## Priority 7 — Select the first calibration customer

Select a customer that has clear client-acquisition pain, fits the category, can provide customer-specific inputs, has decision authority involved, can review three briefs, is willing to act on useful accounts, and can realistically consider the paid pilot.

## Priority 8 — Deliver three real briefs

For each brief, record accounts screened, evidence sources, research minutes, review minutes, variable cost, commercial score, evidence confidence, customer decision, would-contact status, actual-contact status, response, and meeting outcome.

## Priority 9 — Request payment

After a successful calibration:

- Offer the Four-Week Founding Pilot
- Request Rp4 million upfront
- Record objections
- Do not replace payment with indefinite free delivery

## Priority 10 — Choose the initial vertical

Compare recruitment and software or IT services using pain severity, frequency, existing spend, observable signals, actionability, deal economics, decision-maker access, market depth, delivery cost, and willingness to pay.

## Technical sequencing

### Now

- Connect n8n MCP to Codex in a development sandbox
- Define workflow naming, versioning, secrets, and Git export rules
- Do not encode unvalidated scoring or qualification logic

### After discovery

Build Workflow V0 for internal research assistance.

### After real brief feedback

Automate repeated evidence, feedback, and reporting steps.

### After first payment

Productionize recurring monitoring, reliability, audit logs, and customer-specific configurations.

## Explicitly deferred

Do not currently build automated outreach, LinkedIn scraping, candidate personal data, full CRM synchronization, contact-data waterfalls, customer workflow builders, credit-based pricing, large dashboards, multi-industry public interfaces, or autonomous scoring and qualification.
