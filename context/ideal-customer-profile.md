## **Lancara Ideal Customer Profile**

Lancara is designed for B2B service companies that must regularly acquire new company clients.

The company-level ICP is broader than one industry. Individual industries should be served through separate opportunity-intelligence playbooks.

## **Core customer characteristics**

A strong Lancara customer:

* Sells services to other businesses  
* Depends on winning new client accounts  
* Has a reasonably defined market or specialization  
* Earns enough from one successful client to justify paid opportunity research  
* Uses manual or inconsistent account-research processes  
* Has a founder, owner, director, or business-development leader who can evaluate the product  
* Can act on qualified opportunities through email, LinkedIn, calls, introductions, or meetings  
* Is willing to provide feedback on accepted and rejected opportunities  
* Does not already have a mature internal account-intelligence system

## **Primary problems**

The customer may experience:

* Dependence on referrals and personal networks  
* Unclear account prioritization  
* Time-consuming manual research  
* Generic outbound messages  
* Fragmented company intelligence  
* Difficulty identifying credible timing signals  
* Sales knowledge stored in individual employees’ memories  
* Large lead lists with weak commercial relevance

## **Current validation verticals**

### **Specialist recruitment agencies**

Suitable agencies may focus on:

* Technology and digital  
* Engineering and manufacturing  
* Financial services  
* Healthcare and life sciences  
* Executive or specialist search

They should be employer-facing and actively interested in acquiring new client accounts.

Exclude:

* Job boards  
* Candidate-first platforms  
* Inactive agencies  
* Large global recruitment firms with mature systems  
* Firms unwilling to conduct proactive business development

### **Software and IT service firms**

Suitable firms may offer:

* Custom software development  
* Data and AI services  
* Cloud implementation  
* Cybersecurity services  
* Systems integration  
* Managed IT services  
* Enterprise digital transformation

They should depend on B2B projects or recurring service contracts.

Exclude:

* Consumer applications  
* Pure self-service SaaS companies  
* Freelance collectives  
* Very large global system integrators  
* Firms with no clear specialization or portfolio

## **Vertical-selection criteria**

Lancara should compare industries using:

* Severity of client-acquisition pain  
* Frequency of the problem  
* Current time or money spent solving it  
* Availability of observable public signals  
* Ability to approach the identified companies  
* Average value of a successful client  
* Decision-maker accessibility  
* Number of potential customers in the market  
* Cost of producing reliable opportunities  
* Willingness to pay for continued delivery
