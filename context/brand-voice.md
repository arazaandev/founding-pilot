## **Lancara Brand Voice**

Lancara should sound like a trusted commercial research partner.

### **Core personality**

* Calm  
* Precise  
* Evidence-first  
* Commercially aware  
* Practical  
* Honest about uncertainty  
* Human-controlled

### **Writing principles**

#### **Lead with the customer problem**

Explain what the customer is trying to achieve before describing Lancara’s technology.

Good:

> Know which companies deserve your attention and why the timing matters.

Avoid:

> Use agentic AI to automate your GTM workflow.

#### **Separate facts from interpretation**

Clearly distinguish:

* Verified evidence  
* Lancara’s interpretation  
* Unknown information  
* Recommended next action

Do not present an opportunity hypothesis as a confirmed buying intention.

#### **Use clear business language**

Prefer:

* Opportunity  
* Evidence  
* Account  
* Signal  
* Research  
* Qualification  
* Recommended action

Avoid unnecessary jargon such as:

* Autonomous GTM  
* Revenue orchestration  
* AI-powered pipeline acceleration  
* Intent activation  
* Agent swarm

#### **Emphasize quality over volume**

Lancara does not promise large lead lists.

The message should focus on:

* Better-qualified accounts  
* Less wasted research  
* Stronger commercial context  
* More prepared conversations  
* Human-reviewed recommendations

#### **Do not overpromise**

Never guarantee:

* Meetings  
* Replies  
* Revenue  
* Placements  
* Closed deals  
* Confirmed buyer intent

### **Preferred phrases**

* Research less. Approach better.  
* Know which companies to approach—and why now.  
* Evidence-backed opportunity intelligence.  
* Human-reviewed recommendations.  
* A reason to start the conversation.  
* Why this account, why this service, and why now.

### **Tone to avoid**

* Aggressive  
* Hype-driven  
* Spam-oriented  
* Overly technical  
* Certain without evidence  
* Robotic or impersonal
