# Lancara Research Findings

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This document records durable customer, market, competitor, and product learning.

## 1. Opportunity intelligence is the core product

Lancara should not be marketed primarily as generic GTM software or a lead database.

The core customer value is:

> Turning fragmented market evidence into a credible answer about who to approach, why the account matters, why the timing may be relevant, and what to do next.

## 2. Lessons from Clay

Clay demonstrates that several GTM use cases are combinations of the same product primitives:

- Account sourcing
- Data enrichment
- Signal detection
- Customer-specific qualification
- Account research
- Workflow orchestration
- Activation

Important lessons for Lancara:

- Custom qualification is more valuable than generic industry filters.
- Explainable scoring creates trust.
- A canonical account record prevents fragmented intelligence.
- The system should integrate with existing sales workflows rather than replace every tool.
- Customers benefit from experimentation, but Lancara should provide vertical templates rather than a blank workspace.

## 3. Lessons from Bitscale

Bitscale shows how flexible GTM capabilities can be packaged for faster adoption.

Important lessons for Lancara:

- Sell preconfigured playbooks, not technical workflow components.
- Combine several signals into opportunity patterns.
- Convert messy web research into structured fields.
- Include implementation and calibration.
- Operate as an intelligence layer above the CRM.
- Make evidence provenance and freshness visible.
- Sell time to value.
- Avoid forcing customers to understand data credits and enrichment infrastructure.

## 4. Signal stacking is more credible than single-trigger prospecting

One vacancy, funding event, or leadership change is weak evidence by itself.

Lancara should combine customer fit, demand intensity, operational friction, timing events, service-capability match, and external-support plausibility.

Signals support an opportunity hypothesis. They do not confirm buying intent.

## 5. Facts and interpretations must remain separate

Each opportunity should distinguish:

- Verified evidence
- Lancara interpretation
- Unknown information
- Potential disqualifiers
- What should be verified first

This distinction is central to customer trust.

## 6. Recruitment has not yet been properly tested

The first five-company sample was poorly aligned with the intended ICP.

Key learning:

- Platforms and job boards must be excluded before scoring.
- Technology-only specialization may be too narrow.
- Specialist recruitment may include engineering, manufacturing, finance, healthcare, and executive search.
- Business model and sales motion matter more than website labels.

## 7. Recruitment may still be too small as a standalone Indonesian SaaS market

Even if specialist agencies value the product, the reachable domestic market may be limited.

Recruitment could still work as a narrow validation wedge, managed-service segment, Southeast Asian market, or one playbook inside a broader opportunity-intelligence company.

Market depth must be tested separately from customer pain.

## 8. Software and IT services are the strongest comparison vertical

The same core workflow transfers with limited redesign:

```text
Customer ICP
→ Account sourcing
→ Company events
→ Opportunity hypothesis
→ Stakeholder mapping
→ Conversation preparation
```

Potential strengths include a larger number of firms, higher project value, recurring need for new clients, more service categories, and observable expansion, technology, leadership, tender, and hiring signals.

These are hypotheses that require interviews and real output tests.

## 9. Customer interviews must focus on behaviour

Useful questions examine:

- How the last clients were acquired
- Who researched the accounts
- What made the timing credible
- Why accounts were rejected
- How much time and money are already spent
- Who can approve a pilot
- Whether the customer would act on a real brief

Hypothetical interest and compliments are weak evidence.

## 10. Validation requires escalating commitments

Evidence strength increases in this order:

```text
Conversation
→ Detailed feedback
→ Customer-specific inputs
→ Review of real opportunities
→ Actual outreach
→ Payment
→ Renewal or referral
```

A waitlist or positive interview alone does not validate demand.

## 11. Delivery economics are part of product validation

Lancara must track research minutes per account, review minutes per brief, accounts screened per accepted opportunity, variable tool and data cost, total founder time, and effective revenue per delivery hour.

A valuable but permanently manual research service may not support the intended software business.

## 12. Automation should nominate, not decide

n8n and AI can assist with collection, normalization, deduplication, extraction, summarization, draft classification, brief drafting, notifications, and reporting.

Humans should remain responsible for evidence verification, final qualification, commercial interpretation, stakeholder priority, contact-now recommendations, and final outreach angles.

## 13. Customer-facing visualization should show transformation

The clearest visual story is:

```text
Scattered evidence
→ Lancara qualification
→ Prepared opportunity
```

Customer materials should emphasize before versus after, signal stacking, the opportunity brief, time to first value, and pilot measurement.

Avoid leading with n8n diagrams, database schemas, agent architecture, or generic AI imagery.
