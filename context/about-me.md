## **About the Founder**

I am Daffa Arazaan, the founder of Lancara.

My background combines economics, data analytics, business research, and AI-enabled workflow development. I am interested in building practical systems that help businesses make better commercial decisions using public data, structured research, and human judgment.

I created Lancara after observing that many B2B service companies struggle to acquire clients consistently. Their sales teams often depend on referrals, personal networks, fragmented spreadsheets, and time-consuming manual research.

Lancara is being developed to help these companies identify credible business opportunities, understand why an account may need their services, and prepare a more relevant approach.

My role includes:

* Customer discovery  
* Market and account research  
* Product strategy  
* Opportunity qualification  
* Workflow design  
* Human quality review  
* Pilot delivery

Lancara is currently validating its first playbooks manually before automating or expanding them.
